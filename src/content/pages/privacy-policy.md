## Information we collect

This site is a static website. In its current form, it does not require visitors to create an account, log in, or submit personal information to read content.

If and when the following are enabled, this policy will be updated to describe them specifically:

- **Analytics.** We may use privacy-conscious analytics tools (such as Google Search Console or Google Analytics) to understand which pages are popular and how visitors find the site. These tools may use cookies or similar technology and collect information such as your approximate location, device type, and browsing behavior on this site.
- **Email list.** If we offer a newsletter in the future, we will only collect the email address you voluntarily provide, and only use it to send the content you signed up for. You will be able to unsubscribe at any time.
- **Affiliate and outbound links.** Some links on this site lead to third-party booking or travel sites. Once you leave this site, that site's own privacy policy applies to any information you provide there.

## Cookies

This site aims to use as few cookies and tracking scripts as possible. Any analytics or advertising tools we add in the future will be listed here, along with how to opt out where applicable.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
