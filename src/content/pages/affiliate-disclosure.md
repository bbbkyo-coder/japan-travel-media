This site is free to read and is supported by our readers. Here's how:

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site — for example, links to book hotels, tours, activities, or transportation — are affiliate links. If you click one of these links and make a booking or purchase, we may earn a small commission. **This does not cost you anything extra.** The price you pay is the same whether or not you use our link.

We only link to services we believe are genuinely useful for planning a trip to Japan. Affiliate relationships never determine which destinations or experiences we choose to write about — our editorial content is decided independently, and affiliate links are added afterward where relevant.

## Products sold on Etsy

We also sell printable, downloadable travel guides through our Etsy shop. These are paid products that go beyond what's available for free on this site — for example, printable checklists, itinerary planners, and offline-ready reference material. Buying one directly supports the free content on this site.

## Our commitment

- We will never let sponsorship or affiliate relationships change a factual claim in our content.
- If a destination isn't a good fit for a certain type of traveler, we'll say so, even if that means fewer affiliate clicks.
