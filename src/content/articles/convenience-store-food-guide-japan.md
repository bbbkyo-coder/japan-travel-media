## Who This Guide Is For

This is for budget-conscious travelers, anyone curious about Japan's food culture beyond restaurants, or simply anyone who wants to know what is actually worth grabbing during a quick stop rather than defaulting to the first thing they see.

## Practical Tips

- Konbini are genuinely everywhere in Japanese cities, often multiple locations within a short walk of each other — there is rarely a need to go out of your way.
- Most items are priced individually and clearly, making it easy to build a full meal from a few different categories without overspending.
- Staff turnover of stock is frequent, and popular or limited-edition items can sell out, especially in the morning before the lunch rush.

> Specific product names, flavors, and prices change frequently and vary by chain and season — treat the categories above as a guide to what to look for rather than a guarantee of exact current offerings.
