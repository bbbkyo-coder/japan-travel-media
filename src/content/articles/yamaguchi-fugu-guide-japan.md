## Who This Guide Is For

This is for travelers who want to try one of Japan's most famous — and most misunderstood — foods at the place most closely tied to its history, rather than as a novelty item elsewhere.

## Practical Tips

- Fugu is generally at its best (and most in-season) in the colder months, roughly October through March.
- A full fugu course typically includes the fish prepared several ways in one sitting — sashimi, hot pot, and fried preparations are all common within a single meal.
- Reservations well in advance are standard practice for a proper fugu course, especially at a historically significant restaurant like this one.

> Restaurant hours, course pricing, and seasonal availability change over time — treat the above as a starting point to confirm closer to your visit.
