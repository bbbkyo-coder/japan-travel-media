## Who This Guide Is For

This is for anyone visiting Hiroshima who wants to understand its food identity beyond a passing comparison to Osaka, and for anyone planning a Miyajima day trip who wants a specific, reliable seafood stop nearby.

## Practical Tips

- Okonomiyaki in Hiroshima is often eaten at a teppan counter, watching it built in front of you — this is part of the normal experience, not a premium add-on.
- If visiting Miyajima, planning an oyster meal on the Miyajimaguchi side either before or after the ferry crossing is an efficient way to fit it into a day trip.
- Hiroshima's okonomiyaki and oyster scenes are large enough that the two restaurants above are a starting point, not the only options worth considering.

> Restaurant hours, seasonal availability, and prices change over time — treat the above as a starting point to confirm closer to your visit.
