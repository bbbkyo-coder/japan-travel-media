## Who This Guide Is For

This is for anyone who wants to understand Osaka's two defining street foods with a bit of real history behind them, rather than just picking a stall at random in Dotonbori.

## Practical Tips

- Both dishes are inexpensive by Japanese dining standards, making them an easy, low-commitment way to sample Osaka's food culture.
- Okonomiyaki is typically eaten with a small flat spatula (kote) rather than chopsticks at many casual restaurants — take the cue from how it is served.
- Dotonbori has the highest concentration of both dish types in one walkable area, useful if you want to compare multiple shops in a single outing.

> Restaurant hours, menus, and specific offerings change over time — treat the above as a starting point to verify closer to your visit.
