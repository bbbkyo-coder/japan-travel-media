## Who This Guide Is For

This is for travelers routing through Tohoku who want a genuinely distinctive regional dish rather than food that is available everywhere in Japan, and for anyone curious about Japan's branded chicken breeds beyond the more famous wagyu beef equivalents.

## Practical Tips

- Kiritanpo hot pot is generally a shared, table-based dish, making it a natural choice for a group meal.
- Akita City is a reasonable base for this dish, though the Odate area further north is also closely associated with kiritanpo's origins if you want to go deeper into its history.
- Pairing an Akita food stop with a visit to nearby Nyuto Onsenkyo makes for an efficient regional Tohoku itinerary — see our [guide to Japan's best onsen towns](/onsen/best-onsen-towns-japan/).

> Restaurant hours, menu items, and prices change over time — treat the above as a starting point to confirm closer to your visit.
