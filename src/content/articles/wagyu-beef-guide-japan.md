## Who This Guide Is For

This is for anyone planning a wagyu meal in Japan who wants to understand what they are actually paying for, rather than assuming every regional brand name and every grade mean the same thing.

## Practical Tips

- A regional brand name and a numeric grade are not interchangeable pieces of information — a menu might list both, and it is worth knowing that they describe different things.
- Specialty yakiniku (grilled meat) and steak restaurants are generally a more reliable way to try a specific regional brand than a general menu that simply says "wagyu."
- Prices for top regional brands can vary significantly by cut, with items like sirloin commanding a much higher price than less marbled cuts from the same brand.

> Prices, availability, and specific restaurant offerings for these brands change over time — treat brand and grading information above as a stable framework, and confirm current specifics before a special-occasion meal.
