## Who This Guide Is For

This is for anyone visiting Fukuoka who wants to go beyond a single bowl of tonkotsu ramen and understand the district's hot pot culture, which locals treat as at least as important to Hakata's food identity.

## Practical Tips

- Both dishes are traditionally meant for sharing at a table, making them a natural choice for a group meal rather than a solo quick bite (though solo seating exists at some shops).
- Ending a hot pot meal with a bowl of rice or noodles cooked in the remaining broth (zosui or ramen) is standard practice and shouldn't be skipped if offered.
- Reservations are worth making for well-known spots, especially in the evening during the colder months when demand for hot pot is highest.

> Restaurant hours, seasonal availability, and specific menu details change over time — treat the above as a starting point to verify closer to your visit.
