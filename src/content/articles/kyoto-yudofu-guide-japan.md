## Who This Guide Is For

This is for anyone visiting Nanzenji or planning a Kyoto temple day who wants to pair sightseeing with a meal that reflects the same historical and religious context as the temples themselves.

## Practical Tips

- The area immediately around Nanzenji has multiple yudofu restaurants, so this is a workable meal stop regardless of which specific one you choose.
- Portions and course structure are generally built around a leisurely pace — budget more time than you would for a typical lunch.
- Pairing a yudofu meal with a walk through Nanzenji's grounds, including its aqueduct (Suirokaku), makes for a complete half-day outing.

> Restaurant hours, seasonal menu items, and prices change over time — treat the above as a starting point to confirm closer to your visit.
